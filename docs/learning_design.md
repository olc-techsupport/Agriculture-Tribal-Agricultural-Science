# Learning design and outcomes

## Ownership of instructional development

These learning sections are scaffolding for adaptation. Daear Consulting LLC developed the geospatial code, workflows, and documentation under contract to Oglala Lakota College. OLC will develop the full instructional materials, facilitation choices, and curriculum used with its learners.

## Series outcomes

Learners will be able to use an ordered reproducible workflow; trace public data into an artifact; inspect spatial and temporal support, units, and missingness; explain an analytical transformation and limitation; distinguish a proxy from a local observation; separate observation, interpretation, evidence, and authority; and review a small reproducibility improvement.

## Scope

Pine Ridge and Oglala Lakota are the primary instructional focus. Other South Dakota Tribal Nations, counties, climate divisions, or monitoring sites may appear only as clearly labeled regional context. Their inclusion does not represent those Nations, establish permission, or make the repository a multi-Nation operational system.

## Notebook requirements

Every notebook includes objectives, prerequisites and timing, a governance checkpoint, a learner checkpoint, an interpretation protocol, a contribution activity, limitations, and a connection to the next module. Automated tests protect this structure but do not substitute for OLC curriculum development or OLC/OST review.
