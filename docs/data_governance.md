# Draft data governance statement

This repository uses public environmental and agricultural data for reproducible education and regional screening. Public availability does not authorize every use or interpretation of data describing Indigenous Peoples, lands, waters, agriculture, or communities.

The wording and locally applicable frameworks remain pending OLC/OST review. CARE, FAIR, IEEE 2890-2025, Local Contexts, and OCAP® are reference points, not claims of adoption. OCAP® was developed in a Canadian First Nations context; its applicability must not be assumed for OST or other Nations.

No Tribal observational dataset is included or authorized for use. The committed spreadsheets are empty schema examples. Before operational data is collected or processed, the governing Nation must approve the purpose and collective benefit, steward, storage, users, fields, identifiers, location handling, retention and deletion, prohibited uses, incident process, derived outputs, and publication authority.

Until then, the pipeline and dashboard are inactive evaluation prototypes. Configuration values are screening examples—not approved grazing, animal-health, water, drought, or management triggers. Outputs must not be attributed to OLC, OST, or another Nation as approved findings.
